# STEM-AI (STEM2Connect AI Hackathon)

An AI-powered digital business card and STEM research portfolio platform built with **Next.js**, **Supabase**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Google Gemini API**.

## Key Features
- **AI STEM Bio Generator:** Generates concise, role- and research-focused STEM bios using Gemini with a reliable fallback generator.
- **Interactive Smart Card & Slide-Up Research Overlay:** Clean mobile-first profile card with expandable STEM bio & research details.
- **Sustainability Impact Tracker:** Tracks card taps and estimates paper and water savings compared to traditional paper business cards.
- **Live Cloud Backend:** Powered by Supabase for authentication, profiles, and database storage.
